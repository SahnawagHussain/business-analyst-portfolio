#  HRMS Attendance & Payroll Automation with Business Intelligence  
  **Business Analyst Case Study – Sahil Hussain**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study demonstrates how I approach a real-world Business Analyst problem by combining **business analysis, process improvement, and data-driven validation**.

While working on multiple analytics and Tableau projects, I noticed a recurring issue:  
> Dashboards often fail not because data is missing, but because **business processes are inefficient or poorly defined**.

I created this project to showcase how a Business Analyst:
- Understands business problems  
- Translates them into clear requirements  
- Improves processes  
- Validates outcomes using data and dashboards  

---

## 📌 Business Problem

The organization relied on **manual attendance tracking** and **Excel-based payroll processing**, which led to:

- Frequent payroll mismatches  
- High manual effort for HR teams  
- Delays in monthly salary processing  
- No single source of truth for attendance data  
- Limited visibility for management  

### Root Cause (BA Perspective)
The core issue was a **lack of process standardization and automation** across attendance and payroll workflows.

---

## 📌 Business Objectives

Based on the problem analysis, I defined the following objectives:

- Automate attendance and leave tracking  
- Reduce payroll calculation errors  
- Improve payroll processing efficiency  
- Provide real-time visibility through dashboards  
- Enable data-driven decision-making for HR and management

## 📌 Scope Definition

###  In Scope

- Payroll calculation logic

###  Out of Scope

## 📌 Success Metrics (KPIs)

To measure project success, I defined clear and measurable KPIs:

- Improvement in payroll accuracy  
- Reduction in payroll processing time

---

## 📌 BRD Insight

At the Business Requirement Document (BRD) stage, my focus was on **clarity over complexity**.

The goal was to clearly define:
- What the business needs  
- Why the change is required  

without going into system implementation details.

---

## 📌 Process Modeling

### As-Is Process (Current State)
- Attendance recorded manually  
- HR consolidates data in Excel  
- Payroll calculated manually  
- Errors discovered after salary release  

**Challenges:**
- Time-consuming  
- Error-prone  
- Highly dependent on individuals  

---

### To-Be Process (Proposed State)
- Digital attendance captured via HRMS  
- Automated leave approvals  
- Rule-based payroll calculation  
- HR validates and releases payroll  
- Dashboards available in real time  

---

### Swimlane Thinking
Responsibilities were mapped across:
**Employee → HR → HRMS System → Finance**

This helped clearly separate:
- Business actions  
- System actions  

---

## 📌 FRD – Functional Requirements Definition

Instead of over-documenting, I focused on **core, testable system behavior**.

### Key Functional Requirements
- System should capture daily attendance  
- System should allow leave application and approval  
- System should calculate payroll based on attendance data  
- System should generate payroll reports  
- System should provide role-based access  

---

### Business Rules
- Unapproved leave results in salary deduction  
- Payroll is processed once per month  
- Overtime requires HR approval  

---

### Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance:** Fast report generation  
- **Security:** Role-based access control  
- **Availability:** High system uptime  

---

## 📌 User Stories

I focused on **value-driven user stories** instead of volume.

**Example:**
> As an HR Manager,  
> I want to generate payroll automatically,  
> So that salary calculations are accurate and timely.

Acceptance criteria ensured stories were clear for development and testing.

---

## 📌 UAT – Business Validation Approach

I treated User Acceptance Testing (UAT) as **business validation**, not technical testing.

### Validation Activities
- Cross-checked attendance data in Excel  
- Verified payroll calculations manually  
- Confirmed report totals against source data 

My responsibility was to ensure:
> *What the business requested is exactly what the system delivers.*

---

## 📌 Business Intelligence & Analytics

This is where I added the most value.

### Data Modeled
- Employee master data  
- Attendance records  
- Payroll transactions  

### Tools Used
- **Excel** – Initial checks and validation  
- **SQL** – Used only for simple data checks  
- **Tableau** – Dashboards and insights  

---

### Dashboards Created
- Attendance summary by department  
- Payroll cost trends  
- Department-wise salary expense  

These dashboards provided management with **real-time operational visibility**.
