#  HRMS Attendance & Payroll Automation with Business Intelligence  
  **Business Analyst Case Study – Sahnawag Hussain**

---

**Business Context:**  
This project analyzes HRMS attendance and payroll data to monitor employee productivity, leave patterns, and payroll accuracy. The goal is to provide actionable insights for management to optimize workforce efficiency and ensure payroll correctness.

---

## 👥 Stakeholders
- **HR Team:** Track attendance, leave patterns, and compliance  
- **Finance Team:** Verify payroll accuracy and adjustments  
- **Management:** Monitor workforce productivity and make informed decisions  

---

## 📝 Problem Statement
The organization faced challenges with:  
- Tracking absenteeism and leave patterns accurately  
- Identifying payroll discrepancies  
- Extracting actionable insights from large HR datasets  

---

## 🛠 Analysis Performed

### Process Modeling
- Created **As-Is and To-Be process diagrams** to visualize HR workflows  
- Developed **Swimlane diagrams** showing roles and responsibilities in attendance and payroll processes  
- Identified inefficiencies and optimized workflow for payroll and leave processing  

### SQL Queries
All queries are in the `sql/` folder. Each query answers a business question:  
- **Query 1:** Monthly attendance summary per employee/department  
- **Query 2:** Employees with excessive leaves or absenteeism  
- **Query 3:** Payroll validation and discrepancy identification  
- **Query 4:** Leave vs payroll adjustments analysis  

### Excel Analysis
The `Excel-Analysis/HRMS_Excel_Analysis.xlsx` contains pivot tables, formulas, and charts.  
**Highlights:**  
- Attendance summary by employee and department  
- Payroll checks for discrepancies  
- Leave trend analysis  
- Key summary tables for quick insights  

---

### Tableau Dashboards
Dashboards are in the `Tableau-Dashboards/` folder. Each dashboard has screenshots with one-line insights:  
- **Attendance Overview:** Highlights absenteeism patterns across departments  
- **Leave & Payroll Validation:** Displays payroll adjustments and discrepancies  
- **HRMS Executive Summary:** Consolidates key HR KPIs for management  

**KPIs Visualized:**  
- Attendance % per department  
- Payroll discrepancy counts  
- Leave utilization rates  

### User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
- Dashboards and Excel summaries were validated by HR and Finance teams  
- Feedback incorporated to ensure accuracy and usability  
- Ensured dashboards reflect real business needs and decision-making  

---

### BRD / FRD
- **BRD (Business Requirement Document):** Defines project objectives, stakeholders, and expected outcomes  
- **FRD (Functional Requirement Document):** Details functional requirements for reporting, Excel analysis, and Tableau dashboards  

---

## 📈 Key Insights / Business Impact
- Departments with high absenteeism were identified → HR can plan corrective interventions  
- Payroll discrepancies were highlighted → Finance can ensure accurate payments  
- Executive dashboards provide quick KPI insights → Management can make data-driven decisions efficiently  
- Process modeling clarified HR workflow → reduced bottlenecks and improved reporting efficiency
