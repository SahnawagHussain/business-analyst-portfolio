# HRMS Attendance & Payroll Dashboards  
**Tableau – Business Analyst Case Study**

---

## 📌 Dashboard Objective

The objective of these Tableau dashboards is to provide **HR and management teams** with clear visibility into:

- Employee attendance patterns
- Leave behavior and policy compliance
- Payroll validation and cost monitoring
- Key HR efficiency indicators

These dashboards support **data-driven decision-making** by converting raw HRMS data into actionable insights.

---

## 🛠 Tool Used

- **Tableau Public**

---

## 📊 Data Sources

The dashboards are built using cleaned and structured data from the following sources:

- **Employee Master Data**
  - Employee ID
  - Department
  - Role

- **Attendance Data**
  - Employee ID
  - Date
  - Attendance Status (Present / Absent / Leave)

- **Leave Data**
  - Employee ID
  - Leave Type
  - Approval Status

- **Payroll Data**
  - Employee ID
  - Monthly Salary

---

## 📈 Dashboards Overview

---

### 🔹 Dashboard 1: Attendance Overview

**Purpose:**  
To monitor employee attendance and identify attendance-related risks.

**Key Visuals:**
- Attendance percentage by employee
- Department-wise attendance comparison
- Employees below attendance threshold

**Business Value:**
- Helps HR identify absenteeism trends
- Enables proactive attendance management

---

### 🔹 Dashboard 2: Leave & Payroll Validation

**Purpose:**  
To validate leave policy compliance and payroll accuracy.

**Key Visuals:**
- Unapproved leave count by employee
- Leave impact on attendance
- Payroll review indicators

**Business Value:**
- Supports payroll accuracy checks
- Helps HR enforce leave policies

---

### 🔹 Dashboard 3: Management Summary

**Purpose:**  
To provide leadership with a high-level HR performance snapshot.

**Key KPIs:**
- Average attendance percentage
- Employees below attendance threshold
- Total payroll cost

**Business Value:**
- Enables quick management reviews
- Supports workforce planning decisions

---

## 🔍 Key Insights Derived

- Employees with consistent attendance show stable payroll outcomes
- Departments with higher absenteeism require intervention
- Leave approval directly impacts payroll deductions
- Dashboards reduce dependency on manual HR reports

---

## 🧠 How This Supports Business Decisions

- Improves HR operational efficiency
- Reduces payroll errors through validation views
- Provides transparency into attendance and payroll
- Creates a foundation for future HR analytics initiatives

---

## 📌 Business Analyst Perspective

As a Business Analyst, these dashboards were designed to:
- Align with documented BRD and FRD requirements
- Validate business rules using visual indicators
- Enable non-technical stakeholders to interpret HR data easily
